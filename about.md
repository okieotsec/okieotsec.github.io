# About

I'm Jared, an OT security practitioner based in Oklahoma.

My path into security went through the field:

- **Field tech.** Installing and supporting machines on site.
- **SCADA analyst.** Six years building HMI graphics, mapping Modbus registers to tags, writing unit conversions and keeping the servers running.
- **Field communications.** Licensed and unlicensed radio, cellular modems, VSAT, point-to-point links and mesh networks for remote sites.
- **Security.** Information security, then OT security engineering, and now OT security leadership.

I also volunteer on industry advisory boards for technical education and speak to students about OT security careers.

## What you'll find here

- Lab notes from my home OT lab: PLC programming, HMI and historian builds, attacks and detections.
- Practical takes on securing remote and field OT: radio, telemetry and RTUs.
- Resources for people getting into OT security.
